Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 2025

1. Data Collection

We collect only the video URLs you submit and your Session ID. We do not store personal data unless you pay (handled by Stripe/Paddle).

2. Data Retention

All extracted audio and generated Markdown files are stored in ephemeral cache (Redis) and automatically deleted after 1 hour.

3. Third Parties

We use OpenAI/Whisper models locally. No data is sent to external AI providers by us. Payments are processed by Stripe/Paddle.
